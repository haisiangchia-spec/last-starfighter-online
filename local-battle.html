BUILD STATUS // ONLINE VECTOR COMMAND 1.7.2
Interface styled after the local Vector Command game. Current online play supports a 1v1 duel or a 1v14 multiplayer swarm prototype with sealed multiplayer orders, vector movement, weapon selection and subsystem damage. Multi-weapon volleys and directional gunstar laser arcs are implemented; some detailed local-game damage effects and advanced swarm tactics are not yet ported. Asteroid mode includes deterministic collision checks and line-of-sight blocking. Firing effects now replay after movement using the actual resolved hit/miss data; this screen does not claim complete FASA rules fidelity.

SHIP TELEMETRY

PILOT'S FIELD MANUAL

Plot up to five forward movements and three turns. Left and right move forward one hex before rotating. The computer plots the opposing vectors automatically. The Gunstar player declares weapons after movement.

Normal movement and/or firing consumes one power point per active turn. A stationary, non-firing recharge restores five power, to a maximum of twenty.

Laser base 3/range 10; particle base 4/range 9; photon base 5/range 8. Fire uses 2d6 against the adjusted target number. Asteroids can obstruct line of sight.

Tap a ship to inspect its telemetry. Zoom and center affect only your own tactical display.

Local battles use the same tactical combat engine as online multiplayer, including damage restrictions. Death Blossom is available for the Swarm Gunstar.
